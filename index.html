Pulse history

How it works

Each heartbeat changes how much blood is in your fingertip, which slightly changes how much red light passes through. The camera sees that flicker (photoplethysmography, the same idea as a smartwatch). The app filters the signal, finds the peaks and turns the gap between them into beats per minute.
